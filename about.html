ABOUT US

Building Environmentally Conscious Communities

Established in 2021 in Johannesburg, EcoReach Youth Foundation is a registered South African non-profit organisation focused on environmental education and youth-led community greening initiatives across township schools.

Our Mission

To empower township youth through environmental stewardship, sustainable urban agriculture, and climate resilience workshops.

Our Vision

Thriving, environmentally conscious communities driven by active and educated young leaders.

Who We Serve

Our programmes are designed for high school learners, community volunteers, potential corporate donors, and local municipal partners working together to create lasting environmental change.

Stakeholder focus: youth participation, community partnerships, environmental education and sustainable local action.

Leadership & Governance

EcoReach is committed to responsible leadership, transparency, and community participation. Annual governance reports and organisational information can be made available to stakeholders and partners.
